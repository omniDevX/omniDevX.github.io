Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 12, 2026

Overview

This app "Nod: Daily Safety Check-In" respects your privacy. It is designed to function entirely on your device and does not collect, transmit, or share personal data with any server or third party.

Data Collected

The app allows users to voluntarily provide:

  • Name
  • Emergency contact email address
  • Check-in timestamps

How Data Is Used

  • To store and display check-in history
  • To send an email notification to the emergency contact if a check-in is missed

How Data Is Stored

  • Data may be stored locally on your device.
  • If email notifications are enabled, the emergency email address may be transmitted for the sole purpose of sending notification emails.
  • No personal data is used for advertising or analytics.

Data Sharing

Personal data is shared only in the following limited case:

  • The emergency contact email address is used to send notification emails when required by the app’s functionality.

No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing or tracking purposes.

Internet Usage

Internet access may be required to send emergency notification emails. Aside from this purpose, the app does not perform background data collection or analytics.

Permissions

The app does not request access to sensitive device permissions such as:

  • Camera
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Microphone

Data Retention & Deletion

Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the app’s functionality. You may delete all stored data at any time by uninstalling the app.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for use by children under the age of 18 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if new features are introduced. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
